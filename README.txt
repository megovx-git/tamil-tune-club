# Tamil Tune Club — Free Website Starter

## Files
- `index.html`: one-page website and request form
- `style.css`: responsive styling
- `script.js`: request form behavior

## Before publishing
1. Replace `hello@example.com` in `script.js` with your real business email.
2. Replace the same example email in `index.html` (`mailto:hello@example.com`) with your real contact email.
3. Confirm the ₹99 example price, 25-song deliverable, and 48-hour delivery promise are realistic. Change them if needed.
4. Add complete terms, privacy and refund details appropriate to your business.
5. Test on mobile and desktop.

## Important limitations
This is a static demo. It does not take payments, store orders, send automatic emails, or verify transactions. The form opens the customer's email app using `mailto:`. For reliable order intake, connect a reputable form backend; for payments, use a gateway after account approval and link each payment to an order. Never treat an emailed screenshot as proof of payment.

The service is for personalized recommendations and original design work. It does not sell Spotify playlist access, paid placements, streams, or copyrighted recordings.
